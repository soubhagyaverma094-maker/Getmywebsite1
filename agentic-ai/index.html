FAQ

Agentic AI answers

What is Agentic AI?

Agentic AI is an AI system designed to work toward a defined goal and complete multiple steps in an assigned workflow. GetMyWebsite scopes agents around the outcome, the data they may use, and the actions they are allowed to take.

How is this different from a chatbot?

A chatbot answers questions. An agent can collect an enquiry, check details, update a record, notify a teammate, or follow a multi-step process across connected tools.

Do agents replace people?

No. Agents assist operations. Human approval stays in place where the business needs it, and handoff rules are part of the design.

How is Agentic AI priced?

Pricing is based on the agent, tools, workflow, and complexity. We provide a customized quotation after scoping the use case.
