FAQ

AI automation answers

What can AI automations do for my business?

GetMyWebsite can automate WhatsApp replies, website chat, lead capture, CRM updates, appointment reminders, missed-call follow-up, and other repetitive workflows. Exact flows depend on the tools you already use.

How much do AI automations cost?

Pricing depends on the type of automation, complexity, required integrations, and business requirements. We provide a customized quotation after reviewing the workflow.

Which tools can you connect?

Integrations depend on the apps already in use and the access the client can provide. We do not claim a fixed platform list; we scope what can be connected after reviewing the process.

Will a person still be needed?

Yes when the case needs judgement, exceptions, or sensitive handling. Automations cover defined, repeatable steps and can hand off to a teammate.

Ready to take repetitive work off your team?

Start with one workflow. We will connect the tools, set the guardrails and ship an automation you can trust.
