FAQ

Frequently asked questions

  • What does GetMyWebsite do?

    GetMyWebsite is a digital studio that builds signature websites, AI automations, agentic AI systems and AI videos. Each project is designed, launched and scaled as one system instead of separate vendor handoffs.

  • What is a signature website?

    A signature website is a custom-designed, fast-loading, fully responsive site built around your brand instead of a stock template. It ships with an SEO-ready page structure and content you can edit after launch.

  • What AI automations does GetMyWebsite build?

    GetMyWebsite builds WhatsApp automations, AI chatbots, AI voice assistants and business workflows for auto-replies, order updates, reminders, lead capture and approvals.

  • What is agentic AI?

    Agentic AI is software that understands a goal, plans the steps and takes action across your tools, apps and APIs. Unlike a simple chatbot, it completes multi-step work such as finding leads, booking calls and updating a CRM.

  • Does GetMyWebsite create AI videos?

    Yes. GetMyWebsite produces AI-generated videos with characters, voiceovers and custom brand styles from a text prompt, without a camera crew or studio.

  • How long does a website take?

    Timelines depend on scope. Most projects run in one thread - brief, build, launch and scale - so design, automation and media ship together rather than in sequence.

  • Which businesses does GetMyWebsite work with?

    GetMyWebsite serves businesses across India, from local service brands to teams that need AI automation and video content alongside their website.
