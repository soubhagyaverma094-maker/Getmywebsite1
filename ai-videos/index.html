FAQ

AI video answers

What AI videos can GetMyWebsite make?

Promotional clips, advertisements, product showcases, social videos, avatar presenters, voiceovers, and subtitles. Format, style, duration, and deliverables depend on the brief.

Do I still need a camera crew?

Most AI video projects do not need a crew or studio. Live footage can still be mixed in if the brief requires it.

How is AI video priced?

Pricing is based on format, length, style, revisions, and deliverables. We provide a customized quotation after reviewing the campaign.

Which platforms are the videos for?

Videos can be made for social feeds, websites, and ads. Aspect ratios and versions are agreed in the project scope.

Ready to turn prompts into films?

Tell us the campaign, the audience and the style. We will produce the first cut and build a repeatable workflow.
