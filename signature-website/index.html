FAQ

Signature website answers

How much does a signature website cost?

Website development starts at INR 9,999. The final price depends on design, features, functionality, integrations, and project requirements.

What is included in a signature website?

A typical project includes custom design, a mobile-ready layout, service pages, enquiry paths such as forms or WhatsApp, and an SEO-ready page structure. Extra features are scoped per project.

Do I own the website after launch?

Yes. GetMyWebsite ships 100% ownership with no hidden terms. Domain and hosting options can be discussed as part of the project.

How long does a website take?

Delivery time depends on pages, features, content, and integrations. An estimated timeline is shared after the brief.
